Welcome to the Server

Welcome to our official school Minecraft server! This guide will help you understand how to navigate the world, chat with your friends, and earn powerful perks just by playing.

Our server uses a unique, custom-built system designed to make your survival experience as fun and rewarding as possible. Make sure your account is secure by using our built-in login system when you first join!

Playtime Ranks & Perks

You don't need to buy anything to get cool perks here. You automatically rank up and earn powerful buffs simply by spending active time exploring, building, and surviving on the server!

Rank Name Time Required What You Get
Player 0 hours Welcome! You have all the standard survival permissions.
Pro 5 hours Your food goes further! You get a permanent 20% boost to your saturation effectiveness.
MVP 12 hours Time to mine! You are granted a permanent Haste effect.
Elite 24 hours God-tier reach. You get a +2 block bonus to your entity and block interaction range!

The AFK System

To keep our playtime ranks fair, we have an active AFK (Away From Keyboard) detection system. We want to reward players who are actually playing the game!

How it works: If you stand completely still and do not move your character or your head (camera angle) for 2 minutes, you will be marked as AFK.

While you are marked as AFK, your playtime counter is paused. Don't worry, once you start moving around again, your timer will immediately resume!

How to Chat

Because the world is so large and the server can get busy, we use a custom chat system to keep things from getting cluttered.

Regional Chat (Default)

  • The world is divided into 8 equal geographical sections.
  • When you type normally in chat, only players in your section will be able to see your messages. It's perfect for talking to your neighbors!

Global Shout

  • Want to talk to the whole server? Use the command /shout <message> or /s <message>.
  • This will put a shiny [SHOUT] tag in front of your message and broadcast it to everyone online.
  • Note: There is a cooldown on how often you can shout to prevent spamming, which decreases as you rank up.
